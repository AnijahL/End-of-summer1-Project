Games

NovaKnight

Resident Evil 4 is an action-packed survival horror game that follows U.S. special agent Leon S. Kennedy on a perilous mission to a secluded European village to rescue the President's kidnapped daughter. Instead of traditional zombies, Leon faces a terrifying, parasite controlled cult that fights with terrifying intelligence, leading to a tense gameplay loop focused on resource management and crowd control. The experience combines intense third-person gunplay with tactical melee prompts like executing a roundhouse kick after stunning an enemy while forcing you to solve environmental puzzles and carefully organize your weapons in a grid based attaché case to survive the overwhelming nightmare.

RogueWave

Dead Space is a sci-fi survival horror game that follows Isaac Clarke, an ordinary ship engineer trapped aboard a silent deep-space mining vessel overrun by mutated, reanimated human corpses called Necromorphs. As Isaac fights to survive the claustrophobic starship and find his missing girlfriend, the gameplay delivers a terrifying, highly immersive experience centered around "strategic dismemberment," where you must precisely slice off alien limbs using high-tech industrial engineering tools rather than traditional weapons. With a unique screen interface built directly onto Isaac's armored suit, players must manage limited ammunition, solve zero-gravity puzzles, and use telekinetic powers to survive cosmic horrors that threaten Isaac's life and sanity.
