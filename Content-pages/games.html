CosmicJolt

Split Fiction is a clever cooperative action game that follows Mio and Zoe, a sci-fi and a fantasy writer whose draft ideas accidentally merge inside an experimental digital publishing simulation. To expose the corrupt publishers trying to steal their ideas, two players must work in tandem across a constantly shifting split-screen layout that blends lasers and spaceships with dragons and medieval magic. The gameplay constantly introduces fresh mechanics for every single level—ranging from flying colossal fire-breathing beasts to piloting heavily armored sci-fi exosuits demanding constant communication to solve puzzles and fix the program's glitches.
